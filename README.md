- **人在回路的产品** —— 在高影响行动前设置明确的人工确认
- **基于证据的交付** —— 用测试、CI、部署检查和演示结果判断什么已经准备好

# Hi, I'm Lumi Ruvenne

I turn real human problems into testable AI products — from product judgment and system design to working prototypes and evidence-led iteration.

My current focus is AI that helps ordinary people in ordinary situations, with clear boundaries, human review, and a path from demo to something that can be checked.

## What I build

- **AI workflows** — observable, recoverable systems instead of chat-only demos
- **RAG and knowledge systems** — document parsing, hybrid retrieval, ranking fusion, and citations
- **Human-in-the-loop products** — explicit approval before high-impact actions
- **Evidence-led delivery** — tests, CI, deployment checks, demos, and honest limitations

## Selected work

| Project | What it explores |
| --- | --- |
| [ArrivalReady](https://github.com/qrx-joe/ArrivalReady) | An evidence-driven AI acceptance system for visitor reception readiness. AI extracts and evaluates evidence; deterministic rules and human review produce the final Gate. |
| [OneCase](https://github.com/qrx-joe/OneCase) | A community-issue workspace that turns resident feedback into editable, traceable items. Human review is required before formal intake. |
| [EasyPhone AI](https://github.com/qrx-joe/EasyPhone_AI) | A safety-oriented phone coach for overseas Chinese families: one reversible step at a time, with a hard stop and family help card for scam-risk scenarios. |
| [LOOM](https://github.com/qrx-joe/LOOM) | A full-stack AI workflow platform combining a visual canvas, RAG knowledge base, SSE chat, and failure compensation. |
| [TeachOps](https://github.com/qrx-joe/TeachOps) | A multi-agent teaching-design review system where downstream work is blocked until its evidence package is ready. |

More experiments, including the [LifeOPS](https://github.com/qrx-joe/LifeOPS) prototype, are collected on my portfolio.

## How I work

**Observe → Build → Reflect**

I make the product decisions, use AI coding tools to turn them into working systems, and then use tests, CI, deployment results, and demos to decide what is ready. A prototype, a simulated demo, and real-world validation are different kinds of evidence.
